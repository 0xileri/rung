# Week 3 live navigation demo

Here's Rung on Solana devnet, where makers commit capital at company valuations and holders buy downside protection.

OpenAI's curve shows capital and wallet concentration as valuation and size changes update the quote, while the ladder splits capital evenly or toward lower valuations.

On Protect, three hundred dollars produces a two-floor sweep while Review quotes a fifty-dollar partial fill, and these are live previews, not submitted trades.

The activity feed shows our two-wallet tests and keeper-settled claim.

We learned to preserve claim backing during withdrawals, and next we'll test Phantom signing, ladder and sweep transactions, and shared Actions.

## Recording scope

Actual navigation, scrolling and form interactions on the deployed site. The recorder adds a visible pointer highlight. No wallet connection is simulated and no wallet approval is recorded. Existing devnet transaction outcomes are shown from the public app.
