This week, we built Rung: a Solana devnet market where makers commit capital at company valuations.

Holders pay a premium for downside protection. Makers choose a valuation and put capital behind their commitment.

Holders can take partial fills and preview protection across several floors. Makers can withdraw capital that nobody has taken.

We rehearsed with two wallets: creating commitments, taking partial fills, withdrawing unmatched capital, and exercising both claims. Those checks passed.

We also verified the scheduled keeper. It settled an expired claim, returning twenty dollars in mock collateral and the holder's stock without either party returning.

We learned that premium wording must be clear, and withdrawing unmatched capital must preserve the backing of every matched claim.

Next, we will test Phantom browser signing, ladders, sweeps, and shared Actions, then improve the demo using that feedback.
